Senograf Uploader

Senograf Uploader is a private, internal tool. Its only job is to upload videos that we produce ourselves to our own YouTube channels: Senograf, Roxtory, Bixville, Zootown, Behindery, Sineograf History and Animus Audio.

The tool runs on a single computer owned by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload a finished video file to our own channel,
  • set its title, description, tags, category, language, publish time and the altered or synthetic content disclosure,
  • set its custom thumbnail and upload its caption file.

It does not read, collect or store data about other YouTube users, and it is not offered to the public.
